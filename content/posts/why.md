I've been meaning to start writing publicly for a while.

The setup is now done (I built it this week, actually), so here we go.

## Why write?

A few reasons:

**Thinking out loud.** Writing forces you to be more precise than thinking in your head. Half the ideas that feel solid in my mind fall apart when I try to write them down. That's useful.

**A record.** I forget things. Projects I worked on, problems I figured out, what I was thinking about at a particular time. A blog is a searchable log of what I've been up to.

**Connection.** The internet runs on people putting things out there. Some of my favorite people to follow online are just people who write about what they're building or learning. I want to be that for someone.

## What I'll write about

Mostly:
- Things I'm building (apps, side projects, this site)
- Things I'm learning (CS, languages, tools)
- Things I'm reading or thinking about

I'll try to keep posts short and specific rather than long and vague.

Let's see how this goes.
